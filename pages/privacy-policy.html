PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy, Participant Information, Website Infrastructure, and Third-Party Service Providers

This Policy explains how ZEROPOINT handles personal information in connection with its website, enquiries, private engagements and related services.

01. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how ZEROPOINT™ (“ZEROPOINT”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, protects and discloses personal information in connection with THEZEROPOINT.DANCE, enquiries, applications and service requests, private engagements, participant administration, communications and related services.

It applies to personal information handled by ZEROPOINT in connection with those activities.

02. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, location or postcode; information submitted through an application, enquiry or service request; engagement and participant details; communications with ZEROPOINT; and information reasonably needed to administer an accepted engagement.

We may also receive technical and usage information through website infrastructure, cookies, analytics, security tools and similar technologies. The information collected depends on the website features, providers and settings in use.

03. Participant Roster Information — Waiver Administration Only

When a participant’s full name and individual email address are collected for a corporate engagement, Private Dance Soirée, wedding or other organised participant roster, the information is collected to administer the waiver process and related engagement requirements.

Permitted uses include identifying the intended participant; issuing an individual waiver request; confirming completion; maintaining waiver and clearance records; administering participant eligibility and engagement requirements; and managing the associated engagement.

Roster information is not collected for marketing. ZEROPOINT will not add participants to promotional mailing lists or send unrelated marketing communications solely because their details were supplied for waiver administration.

ZEROPOINT does not sell roster information or use it for unrelated commercial purposes.

04. How We Use Personal Information

ZEROPOINT may use personal information as reasonably necessary to review and respond to enquiries, applications and service requests; communicate about proposed or accepted engagements; administer services, schedules and participants; issue, track and retain waivers and related records; process payments and maintain accounting records; protect service security and integrity; comply with legal obligations; resolve disputes and enforce agreements; and operate, maintain and improve its business and website.

Marketing communications may be sent where appropriate and permitted by applicable law. Providing information for an application, participant roster, waiver or engagement does not, by itself, amount to consent to unrelated marketing.

05. Website, Platform & Third-Party Infrastructure

ZEROPOINT may use third-party technology platforms and service providers to operate THEZEROPOINT.DANCE and support hosting, forms, scheduling, communications, security, document administration, payment processing and related business operations.

Information submitted through or processed by these services may be collected, transmitted, stored or otherwise processed by the relevant provider under its own privacy policy, terms, security practices and applicable data-processing arrangements.

ZEROPOINT is responsible for its own handling of personal information within its control. It does not independently control third-party providers’ privacy practices, retention policies, security systems or processing activities beyond the aspects of their services it can reasonably administer or configure.

Independent processing by a provider is subject to that provider’s applicable policies, terms and legal obligations.

06. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed through third-party payment processors or other payment services made available through the ZEROPOINT website or a separate payment arrangement.

Where payment-card information is processed directly by a payment processor, ZEROPOINT does not intentionally collect or retain complete payment-card numbers, security codes or other full payment-card credentials in its own business records.

Payment processing may involve processors, financial institutions, payment networks, fraud-prevention services and other parties reasonably necessary to complete, verify or secure a transaction. Those parties process information under their applicable privacy policies, terms and legal obligations.

07. Third-Party Privacy Policies

ZEROPOINT may rely on third-party providers for website operation, scheduling, communications, security, document administration, payment processing and other business functions.

Individuals should review the privacy policies and terms of any third-party services through which they submit information or with which they interact.

ZEROPOINT does not control providers’ independent privacy practices or processing activities, except to the extent responsibility cannot be excluded under applicable law.

08. Cookies, Analytics & Technical Information

THEZEROPOINT.DANCE may use cookies and similar technologies for website functionality, security, preferences, analytics and other permitted purposes.

The technologies in use may change as website features, configurations and providers change.

Available cookie controls depend on the technology, user settings and applicable legal requirements. Users may be able to manage certain preferences through their browser, device or available website privacy controls. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

09. Information Sharing & Disclosure

ZEROPOINT may disclose personal information to service providers supporting its website and business, including providers of hosting, scheduling, payment processing, communications, security, analytics, document administration and related services.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process or a lawful request; protect the rights, property, safety or security of ZEROPOINT, participants or others; detect, prevent or address fraud, misuse or security incidents; enforce agreements or resolve disputes; or facilitate a merger, restructuring, sale or other business transfer where legally permitted.

ZEROPOINT does not sell personal information.

10. Data Security

ZEROPOINT takes reasonable administrative and operational measures, appropriate to the nature of the personal information within its control, to help protect it against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

ZEROPOINT retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including engagement administration, waiver records, accounting, legal compliance, dispute resolution, security and legitimate business recordkeeping.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of information, its purpose, applicable legal requirements and the need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Requests will be reviewed and handled under the law applicable to the individual and the information concerned. Some information may need to be retained where permitted or required by law, including for legal compliance, waiver administration, accounting or dispute resolution.

ZEROPOINT may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in services, technology, third-party providers, legal requirements or business practices.

For privacy questions or requests concerning personal information within ZEROPOINT’s control, please contact ZEROPOINT through the contact method provided on THEZEROPOINT.DANCE.
