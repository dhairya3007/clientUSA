THE EXPERIENCE

An intimate gathering. An extraordinary shared experience.

Some occasions call for something beyond the familiar dinner, drinks, and conversation. A birthday, milestone anniversary, private dinner, or gathering at a special residence can become an opportunity for guests to participate in a shared activity that feels different from the usual celebration.

The Private Dance Soirée brings guided dance instruction into a relaxed social setting. Guests are introduced to accessible foundations, explore movement with partners, and have time to enjoy the music together.

No previous dance experience is required. The experience is designed to encourage participation without putting guests under pressure to perform.

THE PROGRAMME FLOW

A natural progression from arrival to enjoyment.

01 / ARRIVE

An easy introduction

Guests are introduced to the music, the rhythm, and accessible foundational movements.

02 / PARTICIPATE

Movement and connection

The group develops simple steps and explores partner interaction at a comfortable pace.

03 / ENJOY

Open social dancing

The session concludes with an opportunity to enjoy the music and put the foundations into practice.

YOUR EXPERIENCE

What your experience includes.

  • — One continuous 90-minute private group dance experience.
  • — Capacity for up to 12 approved adult guests.
  • — Salsa (Cross Body), Bachata, and Merengue as the available featured styles.
  • — A choice of one or two featured styles, agreed before the event.
  • — Instruction adapted to the group’s experience and comfort.
  • — Guided foundational steps, partner interaction, and social dancing.
  • — Private-event coordination and discreet handling of your occasion.
