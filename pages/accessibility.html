ZEROPOINT™ ACCESSIBILITY

Our commitment to an accessible digital experience.

ZEROPOINT™ is committed to making its website as accessible and usable as reasonably practicable for all visitors. We aim to provide a clear, considered digital experience and continue to improve the accessibility, readability, navigation and usability of THEZEROPOINT.DANCE.

01.

Website Accessibility

We aim to support visitors by maintaining:

  • Clear and structured page content
  • Consistent navigation and page layouts
  • Readable typography and appropriate visual contrast
  • Meaningful headings and content structure
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation where supported by the website
  • Alternative text for meaningful images where appropriate
  • Forms and interactive elements designed to be understandable and usable
02.

Continuing Improvement

Website accessibility is an ongoing process. ZEROPOINT may review and improve its website structure, content, functionality and presentation as the website develops.

Some third-party services, embedded content or technologies may not be fully controlled by ZEROPOINT and may have their own accessibility limitations.

03.

If You Experience a Barrier

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of THEZEROPOINT.DANCE, please let us know through our private enquiry form.

When contacting us, please describe the page or feature you were trying to access and, where possible, the nature of the difficulty. This helps us understand and address the issue.

04.

Scope

This Accessibility Statement applies to THEZEROPOINT.DANCE and its associated public-facing website pages.

Nothing in this statement is intended to limit or exclude any rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.
