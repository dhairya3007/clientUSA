ZEROPOINT™ — 18+ ONLY

Adult Participation Requirement

01.

Adults Only

ZEROPOINT™ (“ZEROPOINT”) services are exclusively available to individuals aged 18 years or older. Every individual participating in a ZEROPOINT engagement must be at least 18 years of age at the time of participation.

02.

No Services to Minors

ZEROPOINT does not provide dance instruction, choreography, movement instruction, or any other ZEROPOINT services to individuals under the age of 18.

03.

No Exceptions by Consent

The consent, permission, presence, or supervision of a parent, guardian, family member, organization, venue, or other person does not create an exception to ZEROPOINT’s 18+ participation requirement.

04.

Incidental Presence at Engagement Locations

The incidental presence of a person under 18 at a private residence, venue, corporate location, wedding location, or other engagement setting does not, by itself, constitute participation in a ZEROPOINT engagement.

However, individuals under 18 may not participate in the engagement or enter the active instructional area while ZEROPOINT services are being provided.

05.

Age Verification

ZEROPOINT may request confirmation of age where reasonably necessary to administer and enforce its 18+ requirement.

Any individual whose eligibility cannot reasonably be confirmed may be denied participation or excluded from the engagement.

06.

No Unauthorized Participation

Individuals under 18 may not participate in any capacity, including as a substitute participant, guest participant, informal addition to a roster, or observer who enters the active instructional area.

No invitation, informal arrangement, roster omission, or assumption of permission overrides this requirement.

07.

Participant Acknowledgment

By participating in a ZEROPOINT engagement, each participant acknowledges that they are at least 18 years of age and agrees to comply with this requirement and all applicable ZEROPOINT Rules of Engagement.
