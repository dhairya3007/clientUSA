01 / THE PHILOSOPHY

What is ZEROPOINT?

PRIVATE DANCE LEARNING.

We provide a private dance learning experience brought directly to you.

Your environment becomes the classroom. Your objective shapes the engagement. We bring the expertise to you.

02 / OUR EXPERTISE

What We Do

INSTRUCTION & CHOREOGRAPHY

We provide professionally guided dance instruction and choreography designed around the individual, the occasion and the environment.

Instruction may include movement fundamentals, choreography, refinement, Lead & Follow technique, partnering, musicality and practical rehearsal.

03 / HOW WE DO IT

How We Do It

A TAILORED PROCESS

Precisely 60 minutes to learn, practice or refine your passion. Not half of your day travelling back and forth to the studio.

You tell us what you want to achieve. We review the request, determine what the engagement requires, and structure the instruction around your objective.

Once confirmed, we coordinate the mutually agreed day, time and location.

We come to you.
