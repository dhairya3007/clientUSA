Private Dance Introduction

An Introduction to Your Private Dance Experience

"Your Interests. Your Space. Your Own Pace."

Investment $300 per session
Duration 60 Minutes focused instruction
Format Individual / Couple Individual, 2 Participants, or Couples
Location In-Home Private In-Home Instruction
Eligibility Adults 18+ all experience levels

Discover a more personal approach to partner dancing through a dedicated introductory session designed around your interests, experience, and preferred dance style.

Whether you are exploring dance for the first time, refining existing skills, sharing a new experience with your partner, or considering choreography for your wedding, the session is adapted to your individual objectives.

Dance Styles & Focus

Choose from our core dance disciplines:

Salsa (Cross Body) Bachata Merengue Kizomba

Depending on your interests, instruction may include posture, weight transfer, footwork, dance frame, leading and following, musicality, partner connection, and personal expression.

Your Experience Includes

  • ✓ One private, 60-minute in-home session.
  • ✓ Personalised instruction for an individual, two participants, or a wedding couple.
  • ✓ Your choice of dance style, subject to the session's focus.
  • ✓ Technical guidance adapted to your experience and objectives.
  • ✓ A relaxed introduction to our teaching approach.
  • ✓ A discreet, personalised experience without promotional use of your participation.
A Complete Experience in Its Own Right

This is a standalone session. There is no obligation to purchase a package or arrange additional lessons.

Whether you choose to continue your dance journey or simply enjoy the experience, the session is designed around your interests and your time.

An hour dedicated to dance, in your own space and on your own terms.

Strict Discretion: Client identities and personal details remain confidential. We do not use client photographs, videos, testimonials, or identifying details for promotional purposes.
