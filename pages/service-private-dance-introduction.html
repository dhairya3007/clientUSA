THE PHILOSOPHY

A beginning, entirely your own.

Dance can begin with a song you love, a skill you want to explore, or simply the desire to try something different. The Private Dance Introduction is designed to meet you where you are.

Whether you’re taking your first steps, returning to dance, exploring a style with your partner, or considering future wedding choreography, the session is shaped around your interests and objectives.

There is no expectation that you arrive knowing the steps. The focus is on discovering movement, understanding the fundamentals, and experiencing ZEROPOINT’s approach in a private setting.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Considered. Personal. Complete.

  • — One private, 60-minute instructional session.
  • — Personalized instruction for one person or two participants.
  • — Your preferred focus: Salsa (Cross Body), Bachata, Merengue, or Kizomba.
  • — Guidance in posture, weight transfer, footwork, musicality, and partner connection as appropriate.
  • — A session adapted to your experience and learning pace.
  • — A confidential experience with no promotional use of your participation.
