Exceptional Dance Instruction. Delivered to You.

ZEROPOINT brings bespoke partner-dance instruction and curated dance experiences directly to your home or chosen venue.

Your time matters. Rather than travelling to a studio, working around fixed class schedules, or navigating unnecessary journeys, enjoy personalised instruction in a setting that suits you.

Our approach combines technical understanding, musicality, partner connection, and individual expression across four dance styles:

Salsa (Cross Body) Bachata Merengue Kizomba

Whether you wish to develop your technique, create a memorable wedding first dance, host a private dance experience, or bring an engaging activity to your organisation, each service is thoughtfully tailored to its purpose.

Privacy Comes First

Your privacy is fundamental to our service. We do not use our clients, their homes, or their experiences to promote our business. Client identities, private engagements, and personal details remain confidential. We do not use client photographs, videos, testimonials, or identifying details for promotional purposes.

Our commitment is simple: personalised instruction, professional discretion, and an experience centred entirely on you.

Your Space. Your Time. Your Dance. All services are exclusively available to adults aged 18 and over.
