This experience is suitable for individuals, couples, or two participants exploring partner dance for the first time, refreshing past technique, or considering choreography for a wedding or private occasion.

Private Dance Mastery

A comprehensive, multi-session private curriculum designed to cultivate intuitive leading and following, refined body mechanics, musicality, posture, and effortless social confidence across your selected dance style(s).

Programmes are structured across dedicated 60-minute or 90-minute private sessions, scheduled weekly or bi-weekly according to your personal availability, travel commitments, and preferred pace.

Individuals and couples who desire more than memorised routines—those seeking true movement comprehension, authentic partner connection, and lasting physical intuition.

The Signature First Dance

We choreograph across romantic partner styles, contemporary slow dances, upbeat Latin (Salsa, Bachata), elegant ballroom, or bespoke transitions that evolve from intimate to celebratory.

Not at all. Most wedding couples have never danced together prior to their first session. Our methodology builds confidence from your very first hour, focusing on posture and organic connection rather than rigid steps.

Yes. We review your chosen piece, analyse its tempo, phrasing, and structure, and can advise on optimal duration (typically 2.5 to 3.5 minutes) for maximum impact and comfort.

General Booking Information

POLICIES, LOCATIONS & DISCRETION

An engagement is confirmed following our initial discussion, mutual agreement on schedule and location, and receipt of the booking invoice payment.

In accordance with our Terms of Engagement, private sessions may be rescheduled with at least 48 hours’ notice without penalty, subject to calendar availability.

Retainers and session fees are non-refundable once an engagement is confirmed, as dedicated calendar time, travel commitments, and custom preparation are reserved exclusively for you.

Hardwood, smooth laminate, parquet, marble, or polished stone are ideal. Carpeted surfaces can be used for foundational posture work, though smooth flooring is recommended for turns and footwork.

For sessions within primary operational areas, travel is included. For remote private estates, international destinations, or bespoke venue hires, travel expenses are quoted transparently prior to confirmation.

Discretion is central to the ZEROPOINT philosophy. We operate without public client rosters, never publish private session media to promotional channels, and maintain strict confidentiality regarding all client residences and engagements.
