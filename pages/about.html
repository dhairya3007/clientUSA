03 / THE TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

Beyond mechanical steps.

Rather than mechanical steps, the ZEROPOINT methodology prioritises understanding body mechanics, refining lead-and-follow communication, and cultivating genuine confidence.

At ZEROPOINT, the focus extends beyond learning choreography; it is about understanding movement, refining connection, and enabling each individual or couple to make the dance authentically their own.
