About

Discretion & Mastery
Leadership & Pedagogy

Founder & Principal Instructor

Teaching Since 2006

ZEROPOINT is led by a principal instructor whose teaching journey began in 2006, spanning the United Kingdom, India, Africa, Australia, and now the United States.

With an extensive background across partner-dance instruction, technical development, musicality, and movement architecture, instruction is individually tailored to each client's pace, physical intuition, and personal environment. Rather than mechanical steps, the methodology prioritises understanding body mechanics, refining lead-and-follow communication, and cultivating genuine confidence.

Professional recognition includes a bronze medal awarded by the Australian Dancing Society in Latin, Street Latin, and Ballroom categories—grounding bespoke private tuition in verified international discipline and technique.

“Experience that travels. An approach that adapts.”

At ZEROPOINT, the focus extends beyond learning choreography. It is about understanding movement, refining connection, and enabling each individual or couple to make the dance authentically their own—conducted with the absolute privacy, convenience, and discretion that discerning clients require.
