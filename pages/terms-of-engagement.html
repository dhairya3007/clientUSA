TERMS OF ENGAGEMENT

Private Dance Instruction · Wedding Choreography · Private Social Experiences · Corporate Engagements

Please retain these Terms of Engagement for your records.

1. GENERAL TERMS

1.1. Scope of Services

ZEROPOINT™ provides private dance instruction, choreography, movement refinement, and related dance experiences for individuals, couples, private groups, and organizations. Each engagement is structured around the confirmed service, participants, objectives, location, schedule, and agreed scope.

1.2. Enquiry, Review & Acceptance

Submitting an enquiry does not constitute a confirmed booking. ZEROPOINT™ reviews each request individually, taking into account availability, location, requested service, scheduling requirements, environment, and suitability.

An engagement is confirmed only after ZEROPOINT™ accepts the request, communicates the applicable scope and fee, receives the required payment, and issues confirmation.

1.3. Confirmed Engagement

The confirmed engagement governs the service to be delivered, including its duration, participants, session allocation, location, scheduling arrangements, and agreed scope.

No additional session, participant, extension, substitution, credit, refund, or modification is created by implication, unused time, unused capacity, prior practice, discussion, or payment alone. Changes require ZEROPOINT™s express approval and written confirmation where appropriate.

1.4. Location & Environment

The client or organizing party is responsible for providing reasonable access to the agreed location and a suitable environment for the service.

A finished wooden or comparable dance surface is generally preferred. Carpet may restrict turns, spins, and other movements and may require adaptation. Concrete or unsuitable flooring may not be appropriate for activities requiring a suitable dance surface.

ZEROPOINT™ does not provide dance footwear. Instruction may be modified, paused, relocated, or discontinued if the environment is unsuitable or presents a safety, access, privacy, or instructional concern.

1.5. Participants & Guests

Only participants approved for the confirmed engagement may take part. Additional participants, substitutions, or observers require prior approval.

Observers must not interfere with instruction, privacy, access, or the experience. Unused participant places do not create additional capacity, credits, refunds, or future entitlements.

1.6. Professional Instructional Contact

Instruction may involve appropriate professional contact for posture, positioning, alignment, framing, partnering, leading and following, weight transfer, balance, coordination, demonstration, or choreography.

Such contact is limited to legitimate instructional purposes and is conducted professionally.

1.7. Participant Readiness

Participants are responsible for determining whether they are ready to participate and for communicating relevant limitations or concerns before instruction begins.

ZEROPOINT™ provides dance instruction and choreography, not medical assessment, diagnosis, treatment, physical therapy, rehabilitation, or healthcare services.

Participation requirements and liability matters are addressed separately in the applicable participation documents.

1.8. Conduct & Impairment

Professional, respectful, and appropriate conduct is required throughout every engagement. Abusive, threatening, discriminatory, harassing, sexualized, intimidating, or materially disruptive conduct will not be tolerated.

ZEROPOINT™ will not conduct or continue instruction where a participant is visibly intoxicated or materially impaired by alcohol or drugs. Weapons and dangerous items are not permitted in the active instructional area.

ZEROPOINT™ reserves the right to refuse, pause, modify, or discontinue an engagement when conduct or participant circumstances prevent appropriate delivery.

1.9. Scheduling, Punctuality & Rescheduling

Sessions take place at the mutually confirmed date, time, location, and duration. Participant lateness does not automatically extend a session or create additional instructional time.

Private individual, duo, and wedding engagements generally require at least 48 hours' notice for rescheduling.

Corporate rescheduling requests should be made at least seven days before the engagement.

Rescheduling is subject to availability and confirmation.

Genuine emergencies may be considered individually.

1.10. Payment, Cancellation & Refunds

Applicable fees and payment requirements are communicated before an engagement is confirmed.

Payments are generally non-refundable once an engagement is confirmed, except where required by applicable law or expressly agreed in writing. Any cancellation, rescheduling, or replacement arrangement remains subject to the confirmed engagement terms and applicable law.

A request to change an engagement does not automatically entitle the client to a refund, credit, replacement session, or alternative date.

1.11. Independent Practice

Practice between sessions may be encouraged where appropriate. Any practice, rehearsal, repetition, or performance undertaken without the direct supervision of a ZEROPOINT™ instructor is the participant's responsibility.

ZEROPOINT™ does not supervise independent practice or guarantee its results. Applicable participation documents address related participation and liability matters.

1.12. Music & Client-Provided Materials

The client or organizing party is responsible for supplying the music, recordings, images, schedules, venue information, logos, and other materials required for the engagement, unless otherwise expressly agreed.

Music editing, DJ services, licensing, and copyright clearance are not included unless expressly confirmed in writing. ZEROPOINT™ does not assume responsibility for rights or permissions associated with client-provided materials.

1.13. Intellectual Property & Permitted Use

ZEROPOINT™s proprietary instructional methods, movement frameworks, choreography, sequences, exercises, written materials, session structures, Movement Architectureª, Movement Dossierª, and other original instructional or creative materials remain the intellectual property of ZEROPOINT™ or their respective owners.

The client receives permission to use the agreed choreography and instructional materials for the specific personal, wedding, social, or organizational purpose confirmed for the engagement. No transfer of ownership or license to teach, reproduce, resell, publish, distribute, sublicense, or commercially exploit proprietary ZEROPOINT™ materials is implied.

Any broader use requires prior written authorization. This provision does not claim ownership over ordinary human movement or material belonging to third parties.

1.14. Recording, Photography & Publication

Reasonable personal recordings may be permitted for private practice, personal reference, or ordinary wedding and social memories, subject to instructor approval, venue rules, and third-party rights.

Continuous recording, substantial recording of instruction, or recording proprietary teaching methods requires separate permission.

Publicity, advertising, commercial use, endorsement, licensing, or other commercial exploitation of recordings featuring a ZEROPOINT™ instructor requires prior written authorization.

ZEROPOINT™ will not use a client's identity, image, testimonial, recording, or engagement details for publicity or marketing without permission.

1.15. Privacy & Private Environments

ZEROPOINT™ respects client privacy and does not use client identities, experiences, or references for promotional purposes without permission.

Clients are expected to provide a reasonably private and suitable setting. Household activities, deliveries, guests, children, pets, or other interruptions may require a pause, modification, or rescheduling. ZEROPOINT™ does not supervise household members, guests, pets, children, or other nonparticipants.

1.16. Personal Property & Animals

Clients and organizing parties remain responsible for their personal belongings and valuables. ZEROPOINT™ is not responsible for loss of or damage to personal property except to the extent required by applicable law.

Pets and other animals must be securely separated from the active instructional area unless their presence has been expressly approved. ZEROPOINT™ may pause, modify, relocate, or discontinue an engagement if an animal creates a safety, distraction, privacy, or instructional concern.

1.17. Instructor Unavailability & Events Beyond Reasonable

Control If an instructor becomes unavailable because of an emergency or unforeseen circumstance, ZEROPOINT™ will make reasonable efforts to communicate promptly and, where practical, reschedule or arrange another reasonable resolution.

Events beyond reasonable controlÑincluding severe weather, disasters, government restrictions, transportation disruption, venue closure, or emergenciesÑmay affect an engagement. ZEROPOINT™ will make reasonable efforts to communicate and determine an appropriate resolution.

1.18. Right to Refuse or Discontinue

ZEROPOINT™ may refuse to begin, pause, modify, relocate, or discontinue an engagement where conduct, access, environment, privacy, participant circumstances, venue restrictions, technical limitations, or other material concerns prevent appropriate delivery.

1.19. Communication & Electronic Confirmation

Enquiries, engagement details, scheduling, payment, confirmations, and related communications may be handled electronically.

Electronic acceptance or confirmation may document agreement to the applicable terms, subject to applicable law. Changes to an existing engagement require express confirmation.

2. INTRODUCTORY SESSION

2.1. Standalone Experience

The Introductory Session is a complete, standalone private dance experience for an individual, duo, or wedding couple. It allows participants to experience ZEROPOINT™s instructional approach and explore their preferred style.

The session is not a deposit, credit, or automatic gateway to another service. There is no obligation to purchase a package or continue instruction afterward.

2.2. Duration & Fee

The Introductory Session is 60 minutes, with an investment of $300.

Any subsequent engagement is separate and subject to its own enquiry, acceptance, fee, and confirmation.

3. PRIVATE DANCE MASTERY Ñ INDIVIDUAL / DUO

3.1. Package Structure

The standard Private Dance Mastery engagement comprises five private sessions of 60 minutes each, with an investment of $1,500.

Instruction is tailored to the participant's objectives, experience, progress, preferred style, and learning pace.

The available styles are Salsa (Crossbody), Bachata, Merengue, and Kizomba.

3.2. Instructional Focus

Depending on the participant's objectives, instruction may include movement fundamentals, posture, weight transfer, technique, leading and following, partnering, musicality, coordination, movement frameworks, and refinement of selected combinations.

The instructor may adapt the session structure and instructional approach to the participant's progress and preferences without automatically changing the confirmed session allocation or fee.

3.3. Package Limits & Additional Sessions

The package covers only the five confirmed sessions.

Additional sessions may be requested separately and are subject to availability, agreement, and the applicable fee.

Unused sessions do not automatically create a refund, credit, transfer, or extension.

4. WEDDING DANCE Ñ THE SIGNATURE FIRST DANCE

4.1. Standard Engagement

The standard wedding engagement comprises five private sessions of 60 minutes each, with an investment of $1,500.

The engagement includes personalized first-dance choreography of up to 90 seconds, tailored to the couple's music, preferred style, experience, and vision.

Additional Latin or partner-dance styles may be incorporated where appropriate to the music, choreography, and couple's objectives.

4.2. Accelerated Engagement

For weddings taking place less than four weeks after the engagement is requested, an accelerated engagement may be offered at $2,200 for five private sessions of 60 minutes each.

The accelerated fee reflects priority scheduling and route allocation. Scheduling arrangements remain subject to availability and express confirmation.

Sessions may be arranged more frequently where feasible.

However, compressing the instructional schedule does not compress the time needed to practice, rehearse, develop familiarity with the choreography, or become comfortable performing it.

An accelerated engagement does not guarantee a particular performance outcome.

4.3. Additional Parent-Dance Choreography

Additional parent-dance choreography, including fatherÐ daughter or motherÐson dances, is available at $300 per 60-minute session.

A parent-dance engagement requires a minimum of two sessions and a maximum of five sessions, subject to review, availability, and confirmation.

The choreography and session allocation are agreed separately from the couple's first-dance package.

4.4. Participants & Preparation

The confirmed couple and any separately approved participants must remain consistent unless ZEROPOINT™ approves a change.

The couple is responsible for providing the agreed music, attending sessions punctually, communicating relevant wedding arrangements, and practicing between sessions.

Where appropriate and agreed, a final rehearsal may take place at the couple's home to practice spacing, attire, footwear, and movement in a familiar environment.

4.5. Additional Refinement

Additional polishing or refinement sessions may be offered where ZEROPOINT™ and the couple agree that further instruction would be useful. Such sessions are not automatic and require separate approval, scheduling, and payment.

4.6. Choreography Scope & Outcomes

The confirmed engagement covers the agreed choreography and session allocation. Additional songs, longer choreography, extra participants, or materially different requirements require prior review and may involve a revised fee.

ZEROPOINT™ does not guarantee a particular performance, timing, appearance, confidence level, or degree of memorization. Outcomes depend on factors including experience, attendance, practice, cooperation, attire, footwear, music, environment, and available preparation time.

5.1. Engagement Structure & Capacity

The Private Social Soire is a single, continuous, 90- minute private group dance experience for up to 12 approved adult guests, with an investment of $950.

The experience is structured around the confirmed group, occasion, environment, and agreed dance activities. It may introduce guests to Salsa, Bachata, and Merengue in an interactive social setting.

5.2. Organizer Responsibilities

The organizer is responsible for providing suitable space, reasonable access, required venue permissions, and access to a suitable audio system.

The organizer remains responsible for venue equipment, adapters, cables, permissions, and technical support unless otherwise agreed. Compatibility is not guaranteed.

5.3. Participant Roster & Clearance

The organizer should provide the final participant roster, including each participant's full name and individual email address, no later than seven days before the engagement.

ZEROPOINT™ will issue individual participation-document requests. Required documentation must be completed no later than 48 hours before the engagement. The organizer is responsible for communicating the requirements and providing accurate participant information.

Only approved participants who have completed the required documentation may participate. A signed document alone does not authorize an unapproved participant to join.

Additional participants, substitutions, and observers require prior approval. Unused places do not create credits, refunds, or additional capacity.

5.4. Active Area & Conduct

The active instructional area must remain free of alcoholic beverages and dangerous items. Participants may not hold glasses, bottles, cups, or alcoholic beverages while dancing. Beverages should remain outside the active movement area.

Professional conduct and the general participation requirements in Section 1 apply throughout the engagement.

5.5. Recording & Practice

Reasonable personal and social recordings may be permitted, subject to instructor approval, venue rules, and third-party rights. Commercial, promotional, advertising, endorsement, licensing, or other commercial use requires prior written authorization.

Practice outside the confirmed session is the participant's responsibility. ZEROPOINT™ does not guarantee a particular group performance, skill level, or outcome.

The Corporate Connection provides tailored dance and movement experiences for organizations, executive gatherings, corporate retreats, and team-building events.

Each engagement is defined by the confirmed program, objectives, duration, participant capacity, date, location, structure, and fee.

Corporate pricing and format are determined individually and confirmed before the engagement is accepted.

6.2. Organizational Contact & Responsibilities

The organization must designate one responsible contact for communication and provide accurate participant information.

The organization is responsible for securing the venue, suitable flooring and space, venue permissions, reasonable access, participant communication, and compliance with relevant building or third-party restrictions.

The organization should provide the final participant roster, including full names and individual email addresses, no later than seven days before the engagement.

ZEROPOINT™ will issue individual participation-document requests, which must be completed no later than 48 hours before the engagement.

Only approved participants who have completed the required documentation may participate.

6.3. Participant Capacity & Additional Sessions

The authorized participant capacity is specified in the confirmed corporate engagement.

If a request exceeds that capacity, ZEROPOINT™ may consider dividing the engagement into two consecutive 45-minute sessions on the same date, subject to availability, operational requirements, and written confirmation.

This arrangement is not automatic. If a suitable consecutive arrangement is unavailable, ZEROPOINT™ may offer a separate session date, subject to availability and the applicable fee.

No additional participant or revised session structure is authorized until expressly confirmed by ZEROPOINT™

6.4. Audio & Technical Requirements

Any necessary cables, adapters, venue permissions, or IT support remain the organizationÕs responsibility unless otherwise agreed. ZEROPOINT™ does not guarantee compatibility with proprietary venue systems.

6.5. Program Changes & Scheduling

Sessions take place at the confirmed date, time, location, and duration. Late arrival does not extend the session.

Changes to the program, participant capacity, duration, format, location, or session structure require prior approval and, where applicable, revised confirmation and fees.

Corporate rescheduling requests should be made at least seven days before the engagement. Requests made with less notice may be declined or handled under the confirmed cancellation and rescheduling terms. Genuine emergencies may be considered individually.
