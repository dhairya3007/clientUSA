Curriculum 9 Core Pillars holistic technical mastery

Designed for individuals and couples seeking personalised instruction, technical development, and a deeper understanding of partner dancing across our 9 core technical pillars.

Rather than simply memorising sequences, our structured approach equips you with the posture, body mechanics, and lead-and-follow intuition to dance confidently with any partner to any music.

The 9 Pillars of Technical Mastery

Our holistic curriculum develops your dance from internal mechanics to effortless social expression:

Pillar 01
Posture & Alignment

Foundations of balance, poise, and effortless natural movement.

Pillar 02
Weight Transfer & Balance

Controlled transitions and fluid body dynamics from step to step.

Pillar 03
Footwork & Timing

Rhythmic precision and style-specific foot placement.

Pillar 04
Dance Frame & Connection

Upper-body structure, tension balance, and clear partner communication.

Pillar 05
Leading & Following

Clear physical signalling, active listening, and responsive partnering.

Pillar 06
Musicality & Phrasing

Hearing instruments, syncopation, breaks, and musical nuances.

Pillar 07
Turn Technique & Spins

Spotting, core engagement, and executing stable single and double turns.

Pillar 08
Spatial Awareness & Flow

Floorcraft, movement dynamics, and command of personal space.

Pillar 09
Personal Expression & Styling

Developing your own natural identity, elegance, and flavour on the floor.
