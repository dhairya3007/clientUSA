05 · Organisations & Teams

The Corporate Connection

Building Trust. Strengthening Teamwork. Connecting Through Dance.

"Communication. Adaptability. Collaboration in Motion."

Investment Bespoke proposal upon request
Duration Flexible 90 mins to full day
Audience Teams & Orgs teams & leadership
Format On-Site Office, Venue, or Retreat
Eligibility Adults 18+ all fitness levels

Effective teamwork relies on trust, clear communication, and adaptability. The Corporate Connection offers an engaging alternative to conventional team-building activities, using partner dance to explore collaboration in motion.

Partner dancing requires active listening, mutual respect, precise non-verbal signalling, and real-time responsiveness. Through structured exercises, colleagues experience the dynamics of leadership, supportive followership, and shared rhythm in a disarming, enjoyable environment.

Corporate Workshop Formats

Customised programmes calibrated to your team size and event objectives:

Format 01
The 90-Minute Icebreaker

An energetic, engaging session designed to refresh attendees during conferences, quarterly kickoffs, or company retreats.

Format 02
Leadership & Trust Lab

A half-day deep-dive focused on non-verbal communication, leading, following, and cross-functional empathy.

Format 03
Corporate Retreat Experience

A full-day immersive programme combining dance mechanics, team choreography challenges, and an evening social showcase.
