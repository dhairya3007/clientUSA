Experience

ZEROPOINT is led by an instructor whose teaching journey began in 2006, spanning the United Kingdom, India, Africa, Australia, and now the United States.

With a background in partner-dance instruction, technical development, and musicality, the approach adapts to each client's objectives, experience, and preferred style. Recognition includes a bronze medal awarded by the Australian Dancing Society in Latin, Street Latin, and Ballroom categories.
