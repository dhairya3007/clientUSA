General Booking Information

POLICIES, LOCATIONS & PARTICIPATION

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. ZEROPOINT must accept the request, communicate the applicable service scope and fee, receive the required payment, and issue confirmation.

Private individual, duo, and wedding engagements generally require at least 48 hours' notice for rescheduling requests. Corporate engagements generally require at least seven days' notice. All changes are subject to availability, confirmation, and the applicable Terms of Engagement. Other engagement-specific arrangements will be communicated as part of the confirmation.

Payments are generally non-refundable once an engagement is confirmed, except where required by applicable law or expressly agreed in writing. Please review the applicable Terms of Engagement before booking.

A suitable, accessible environment with safe and appropriate flooring is required. A finished wooden or comparable dance surface is generally preferred.
