TERMS OF ENGAGEMENT

Private Dance Instruction · Wedding Choreography · Private Social Experiences · Corporate Engagements

Please retain these Terms of Engagement for your records.

01 / TERMS & CONDITIONS

GENERAL TERMS

APPLIES TO ALL ZEROPOINT ENGAGEMENTS

1.1 Scope of Services

ZEROPOINT™ provides private dance instruction, choreography, movement coaching and related dance experiences for individuals, duos, private groups and organisations. Each engagement is limited to the service, participants, duration, location, schedule and scope confirmed in writing.

1.2 Enquiry, Acceptance & Confirmation

Submitting an enquiry does not create a booking. ZEROPOINT reviews each request individually, taking account of the requested service, location, date, availability, venue and proposed arrangements. A booking is confirmed only when ZEROPOINT has accepted the request, communicated the service scope and applicable fee, received the required payment, and issued confirmation. Do not treat an enquiry, provisional discussion or payment alone as confirmation.

1.3 Changes to a Confirmed Engagement

The confirmation records the agreed service, participants, session allocation, duration, location, fee and scheduling arrangements. Any change to these matters—including additional participants, substitutions, extra time, extra sessions or a different service scope—requires ZEROPOINT’s prior agreement and confirmation. Unused time, unused capacity, informal discussion or an unrequested change does not create an entitlement to an extension, additional service, credit, refund or replacement session.

1.4 Location, Access & Flooring

The client or organising party must provide reasonable access to the agreed location and a suitable, safe instructional environment. A finished wooden or comparable dance surface is generally preferred. Carpet may restrict turns and other movements; concrete or otherwise unsuitable flooring may require adaptations or make instruction inappropriate. ZEROPOINT does not provide dance footwear. Instruction may be adapted, paused, relocated or discontinued if the space, flooring, access or other environmental conditions are unsuitable.

1.5 Participants, Guests & Observers

Only participants approved for the confirmed engagement may take part. Additional participants, substitutions and observers require prior approval. The confirmed capacity must not be exceeded. Observers must not interfere with instruction, participant privacy, access or the agreed experience. An unused place does not create a credit, refund, transfer or additional capacity.

1.6 Instructional Contact & Participant Readiness

Reasonable professional contact may be used for instructional purposes, including posture, alignment, positioning, framing, partnering, leading and following, balance, weight transfer, demonstration and movement technique. Participants are responsible for deciding whether they are comfortable taking part and for communicating relevant limitations or concerns before or during the engagement. ZEROPOINT provides dance instruction and choreography, not medical assessment, diagnosis, treatment, physical therapy, rehabilitation or healthcare services.

1.7 Conduct, Safety & Impairment

All participants are expected to behave respectfully and follow reasonable instructional and safety directions. Abusive, threatening, discriminatory, harassing, sexualised, intimidating or materially disruptive conduct is not acceptable. ZEROPOINT will not begin or continue instruction where a participant is visibly intoxicated or materially impaired by alcohol or drugs. Weapons and dangerous items must remain outside the active instructional area. ZEROPOINT may refuse, pause, modify or discontinue an engagement where conduct or participant circumstances prevent safe and appropriate delivery.

1.8 Scheduling, Punctuality & Rescheduling

Sessions take place at the confirmed date, time, location and duration. Participant lateness does not automatically extend a session or create additional instructional time. Private individual, duo and wedding rescheduling requests generally require at least 48 hours’ notice. Corporate rescheduling requests should generally be made at least seven days before the engagement. All changes are subject to availability and confirmation. Genuine emergencies may be considered individually.

1.9 Fees, Payment, Cancellation & Refunds

Fees and payment requirements are communicated before confirmation. Payments are generally non-refundable once an engagement is confirmed, except where required by applicable law or expressly agreed in writing. Unused sessions, missed attendance or an unavailable participant do not automatically qualify for a refund, credit, transfer, extension or replacement session. Cancellation and change requests are handled under the confirmed engagement arrangements and applicable law. Nothing in these Terms limits a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

1.10 Independent Practice

Practice or rehearsal undertaken outside a scheduled session is not supervised by ZEROPOINT and remains the participant’s responsibility. ZEROPOINT does not guarantee the results of independent practice.

1.11 Music & Client-Provided Materials

Unless otherwise agreed, the client or organising party supplies the agreed music and any required venue information, schedules, images, logos or other materials. Music editing, DJ services, music licensing and copyright clearance are not included unless expressly confirmed in writing. The client or organising party is responsible for ensuring that it has the rights and permissions needed to provide or use its materials.

1.12 Intellectual Property & Permitted Use

ZEROPOINT’s original choreography, instructional methods, movement frameworks, exercises, written materials, session structures, Movement Architecture™, Movement Dossier™ and other original creative or instructional materials remain the intellectual property of ZEROPOINT or their respective owners. The client may use the agreed choreography and materials for the specific personal, wedding, social or organisational purpose confirmed for the engagement. No ownership transfer or permission to teach, reproduce, resell, publish, distribute, sublicense or commercially exploit ZEROPOINT materials is granted unless authorised in writing. This clause does not claim ownership of ordinary human movement or third-party material.

1.13 Photography, Recording & Publicity

Reasonable personal photographs or recordings may be permitted with the instructor’s approval, subject to venue rules and the privacy and rights of other participants. Substantial recording of instruction or proprietary teaching methods requires separate permission. Photography or recording for public social media, websites, advertising, endorsements, licensing or other promotional or commercial use must be agreed with ZEROPOINT in advance. The client or organiser is responsible for obtaining appropriate participant and third-party consent. ZEROPOINT will not use a client’s identity, image, testimonial, recording or engagement details for publicity or marketing without permission.

1.14 Privacy & Private Environments

ZEROPOINT treats client identities, private engagements and personal details discreetly and does not use client experiences or references for promotion without permission. The client or organiser should provide a reasonably private and suitable setting. Household activities, deliveries, guests, children, pets or other interruptions may require a pause, modification or rescheduling. ZEROPOINT does not supervise household members, guests, pets, children or other nonparticipants.

1.15 Personal Property & Animals

Clients and organising parties remain responsible for their personal belongings and valuables, subject to any responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded. Pets and other animals must be securely separated from the active instructional area unless their presence has been expressly approved. ZEROPOINT may pause, modify, relocate or discontinue an engagement if an animal creates a safety, distraction, privacy or instructional concern.

1.16 Unavailability & Events Beyond Reasonable Control

If an instructor becomes unavailable because of an emergency or unforeseen circumstance, ZEROPOINT will make reasonable efforts to notify the client promptly and, where practical, discuss rescheduling or another reasonable resolution. Severe weather, disasters, government restrictions, transportation disruption, venue closure and other events beyond reasonable control may affect an engagement. ZEROPOINT will make reasonable efforts to communicate and agree an appropriate practical response.

1.17 Right to Refuse, Pause or Discontinue

ZEROPOINT may refuse to begin, pause, modify, relocate or discontinue an engagement where conduct, access, environment, privacy, participant circumstances, venue restrictions, technical limitations or another material concern prevents appropriate delivery.

1.18 Electronic Communications & Amendments

Enquiries, service details, scheduling, payments, confirmations and related communications may be handled electronically.

Acceptance of these Terms and confirmation of an engagement may also be communicated electronically. Such communications may serve as evidence of the parties’ agreement, subject to applicable law.

ZEROPOINT may update these Terms from time to time for future engagements. The version provided to the client at the time an engagement is confirmed will apply to that engagement, unless the parties subsequently agree to a change in writing or a different requirement applies under applicable law.

02 / TERMS & CONDITIONS

SERVICE-SPECIFIC TERMS

THE CONFIRMED SERVICE DEFINES THE ENGAGEMENT

2.1 Private Dance Introduction

The Private Dance Introduction is a standalone 60-minute experience for one individual or two participants. The investment is $300. The session is tailored to the agreed focus and may explore Salsa (Cross Body), Bachata, Merengue or Kizomba. It is not a deposit or credit towards another service. There is no obligation to book further sessions. Any subsequent session or programme is a separate engagement, subject to a new enquiry, availability review, fee and confirmation.

2.2 Private Dance Mastery

The standard Private Dance Mastery programme comprises five private sessions of 60 minutes each, with an investment of $1,500. It is available to an individual or duo. Instruction is tailored to the participants’ objectives, experience, progress, preferred style and learning pace. Styles may include Salsa (Cross Body), Bachata, Merengue and Kizomba. The agreed focus may include fundamentals, posture, weight transfer, technique, partnering, leading and following, musicality, coordination and selected movement combinations. The programme develops selected skills within the confirmed scope; it does not promise mastery of every style or a particular result. Additional sessions require separate agreement, availability and payment. Unused sessions do not automatically create a refund, credit, transfer or extension.

2.3 The Signature First Dance

The Standard Package comprises five private sessions of 60 minutes each, with an investment of $1,500. It includes personalised first-dance choreography of up to 90 seconds maximum, tailored to the couple’s agreed music, experience, preferred style and vision. The Standard Package is intended for weddings typically 6–12 weeks away. Where the wedding is less than four weeks after the engagement is requested, an accelerated package may be offered for $2,200, subject to availability and confirmation. The accelerated fee reflects priority scheduling across the five sessions; sessions may be arranged more frequently where feasible. It does not guarantee a particular performance outcome.

Additional Latin or other partner-dance styles may be incorporated where appropriate to the music and agreed choreography. Additional songs, choreography beyond 90 seconds, extra participants or materially different requirements require prior review and may involve a revised fee.

Additional father–daughter or mother–son choreography may be booked at $300 per 60-minute session, with a minimum of two and a maximum of five sessions, subject to review, availability and confirmation. This is separate from the couple’s package.

The couple supplies the agreed music and is responsible for attending sessions punctually and practising between sessions. Music editing, DJ services, licensing and copyright clearance are excluded unless expressly agreed. A particular performance, timing, appearance, confidence level or memorisation outcome is not guaranteed.

2.4 The Private Dance Soirée

The Private Dance Soirée is one continuous 90-minute private group experience for up to 12 approved adult guests. The investment is $950. The experience may introduce guests to Salsa (Cross Body), Bachata and Merengue. One or two styles may be selected as the session’s focus. No previous dance experience is required.

The host or organiser must provide a suitable location, safe and appropriate flooring, reasonable access and necessary venue permissions. Available audio facilities should be discussed with ZEROPOINT in advance so the music and sound arrangements can be confirmed. Any venue equipment, access permissions or technical support that the organiser agrees to provide remains the organiser’s responsibility.

The final participant roster, including each guest’s full name and individual email address, must be supplied at least seven days before the engagement. Where required, participation documents must be completed no later than 48 hours before the engagement. Only approved participants who have completed the applicable requirements may participate. Additional guests, substitutions and observers require prior approval. The confirmed capacity may not be exceeded without ZEROPOINT’s express authorisation. If a confirmed guest cannot attend, their place is forfeited and does not qualify for a credit, refund or replacement session.

Alcoholic beverages, glasses, bottles, cups and dangerous items must remain outside the active instructional area. Participants must not hold drinks while dancing.

Reasonable personal photographs and recordings may be permitted with instructor approval, subject to venue rules and the privacy and rights of other participants. Commercial or promotional use requires prior written authorisation.

2.5 The Corporate Connection

The Corporate Connection is a bespoke dance and movement experience for organisations, workplace events, executive gatherings and retreats. Pricing and programme structure are determined individually according to objectives, duration, participant capacity, date, location, format and operational requirements. The proposal and fee are confirmed before acceptance.

The organisation must designate a responsible contact and provide accurate participant information. It is responsible for arranging a suitable venue, safe and appropriate flooring, reasonable access, venue permissions, participant communication and any organisational requirements. The final roster, including full names and individual email addresses, should be supplied at least seven days before the engagement. Required participation documents must be completed no later than 48 hours before the engagement. Only approved participants who have completed the applicable requirements may participate.

If the requested group exceeds the agreed capacity, ZEROPOINT may consider two consecutive structured sessions on the same date, subject to availability and written confirmation. Alternatively, a separate date or revised fee may be proposed. No change is authorised until confirmed.

Access to the venue’s existing audio system may be arranged where available and permitted. Any necessary equipment, cables, adapters, venue permissions or technical support must be addressed by the organisation unless otherwise agreed.

Corporate rescheduling requests should generally be made at least seven days before the engagement. Requests with less notice may be declined or handled under the confirmed terms; genuine emergencies may be considered individually.

Company photography or recording is subject to appropriate participant consent and privacy considerations. Any public social media, paid advertising or other promotional use must be agreed with ZEROPOINT in advance.

Specific performance, skill, cohesion, employee response, team-building or organisational outcomes are not guaranteed.
